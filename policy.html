Artificial Intelligence in Cybersecurity: Innovation and Human Responsibility

Technology and Responsibility

Artificial intelligence is becoming an important tool in cybersecurity. It can help security professionals review large amounts of information, identify suspicious activity, and organize digital evidence. However, the accuracy of AI-generated findings depends on the quality of the information provided.

MY PERSPECTIVE

AI should assist investigators, not replace human judgment.

Through my research on AI-assisted ransomware incident response and digital forensics, I explored how artificial intelligence can help investigators review security evidence and identify suspicious activity. However, AI-generated findings must be verified against actual evidence before conclusions are made.

Risks & Recommendations

Accuracy and Reliability

AI can make mistakes when analyzing security evidence. Investigators should check AI findings against the original records before making decisions.

Incomplete Evidence

AI cannot always provide accurate conclusions when important information is missing. Security analysts should review the available evidence and identify any gaps in the investigation.

Human Responsibility

AI should support cybersecurity professionals, not replace their judgment. Investigators are responsible for verifying findings and making final decisions.
